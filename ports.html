Dolphintech operates at all major seaports, oil terminals and offshore locations across Nigeria — from Lagos to the Niger Delta and beyond.

Port Coverage

Every Major Nigerian Port

We maintain active operations and regulatory relationships at all major Nigerian seaports, terminals and anchorages.

⚓

Apapa Port Complex

Lagos — Headquarters

Nigeria's largest port and our headquarters. Full ship agency, customs clearing, and cargo services. Direct access to NPA, Customs, Immigration and all port regulatory bodies.

🏭

Tin Can Island Port

Lagos

Major container and RoRo terminal in Lagos. Dolphintech handles vessel calls, cargo clearing and customs documentation for all cargo types at Tin Can.

🛢️

Onne Port Complex

Rivers State

Major oil & gas and deep-sea port serving the Niger Delta. Full ship agency and offshore vessel support, including FPSO tendering and supply base operations.

🚢

Port Harcourt Port

Rivers State

Ship agency and cargo services at Port Harcourt main port. Close proximity to major oil company offices and Niger Delta operations.

🌊

Bonny / NLNG Terminal

Rivers State

Vessel agency support for LNG, crude and product tankers calling at Bonny Island, including NLNG loading terminal and Shell Bonny export terminal operations.

⛵

Warri Port

Delta State

Ship agency at Warri port and inland waterway terminals. Petroleum product loading and barge operations on the Warri/Escravos corridor.

🏗️

Calabar Port

Cross River State

Full ship agency and customs clearing at Calabar deep-sea port. Handling bulk cargo, agri-products and general cargo vessels at Nigeria's eastern seaboard gateway.

🔧

Sapele Port

Delta State

Vessel agency and cargo services at Sapele inland port. Timber, agricultural and general cargo handling on the Benin River.

Offshore & STS Locations

Nigerian Waters

STS and offshore vessel support at all standard Nigerian anchorage areas including Lome STS zone, Offshore Lagos, Offshore Bonny, Forcados and Escravos.
