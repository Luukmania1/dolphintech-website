Privacy Policy

How Dolphintech Global Services Limited collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: November 2024

1. Information We Collect

When you submit the contact/quote form on our website, we collect the name, company name, phone number, email address and details you provide. We do not collect any other personal data automatically beyond standard server access logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your enquiry, provide the maritime services you have requested, and communicate with you about your vessel or cargo requirements. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies

This website uses a single functional cookie to remember that you have accepted this cookie notice. We do not use tracking, advertising or analytics cookies unless you have consented. You may clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

4. Data Retention

Enquiry data submitted through the contact form is retained for a maximum of 24 months or until the relevant business relationship ends, whichever is sooner.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at info@dolphintechltd.com.ng.
